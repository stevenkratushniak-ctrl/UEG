# Pricing And Offer

This is a simple first commercial offer that keeps the product easy to understand on launch day.

## Community

**Price:** Free

**Includes:**

- Source checkout
- Self-build from source
- Public docs and quick demo
- No support commitment

## Founding License

**Price:** $99 one-time

**Includes:**

- Prebuilt release binaries
- Commercial use
- Checksum file and launch docs
- Updates for 12 months
- Email support

## Team License

**Price:** $499 one-time

**Includes:**

- Everything in Founding License
- Use by one internal team
- Shared team inbox support
- Priority bug triage
- Updates for 12 months

## Enterprise / Support

**Price:** Custom

**Includes:**

- Everything in Team License
- Onboarding call
- Procurement-friendly quote flow
- Priority response window
- Optional support retainer

## Simple launch guidance

- Put Community and Founding License on the landing page first
- Keep Team and Enterprise available through contact
- Do not invent a SaaS roadmap before demand exists
