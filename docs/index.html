03

Verify

Export evidence another party can check with independent identity and lifecycle inputs.

LIMITS STAY CLOSE TO THE CLAIM

Evidence is useful only when its boundary remains visible.

  • UEG is not process containment or an operating-system sandbox.
  • It does not prove the host, operator, environment, or clock was honest.
  • It does not observe every syscall, network action, filesystem effect, or side effect.
  • A B+ identity proves pseudonymous ledger continuity, not real-world identity.
  • Offline verification cannot prove that no newer lifecycle event exists.
  • Only Windows x64 and Linux amd64 are qualified distribution targets.
  • Linux arm64, macOS, ARM, and WSL are not qualified distribution targets.
  • The engineering release is unsigned and has not received an independent security review.
