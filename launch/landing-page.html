Demo in under a minute

Run a command, save a receipt, replay it, then tamper with the receipt and watch UEG detect the change.

1. Run with receipt

./ueg --receipt ./demo-receipt.json /bin/echo hello-from-ueg

2. Replay and confirm

./ueg --replay ./demo-receipt.json

3. Tamper and prove it

./ueg --replay ./demo-receipt-tampered.json

FAQ

Is UEG a shell replacement?

No. It is a CLI that adds receipts, replay, and tamper detection around command execution.

Does UEG sandbox commands?

No. It improves the execution record but does not claim OS-level isolation.

Does UEG require a service or account?

No. UEG runs locally as a standalone binary.
